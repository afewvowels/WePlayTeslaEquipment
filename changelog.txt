Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026. 11. 14
  Changes:
    - Now requires the WePlay Beam Library (0.16.0 or later). The lightning's shapes, twitching,
      branches and moods, the beam pieces and the visual beam prototypes now come from the library,
      so the same lightning engine can be shared with other WePlay mods. The look and performance are
      unchanged.
    - Visual beam prototypes are renamed to <family>-<tint>-<speed>[-first|-last|-single], e.g.
      wp-tesla-arc-violet-dim-fast-first.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.4.2
Date: 2026. 11. 14
  Graphics:
    - Bolts now come in four tints (white, ice, blue, violet), so overlapping lightning mixes colours
      instead of all adding up to flat white.
    - Added depth: bolts can be bright or dim, and chain jumps are usually dim so they sit behind the
      main shots.
    - Tighter, softer glow on the lightning and spark bursts, so big fights keep the gaps between
      bolts instead of fogging into one glowing mass.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.4.1
Date: 2026. 11. 14
  Features:
    - Added a "Lightning visuals" map setting. Quality keeps the full lightning. Performance drops the
      branches, draws fewer and longer pieces and redraws less often, creating about a quarter as
      many beams. Damage is the same in both.
  Locale:
    - Added names and descriptions for the new setting.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026. 11. 14
  Graphics:
    - Lightning now has moods: it thrashes, settles and twitches at an irregular pace instead of
      redrawing at a fixed rate. Calm stretches hold their shape and only twitch.
    - The sprite flicker speeds up and slows down with the mood.
    - Long dead-end branches now split off the bolt and fade out, sometimes with a smaller branch of
      their own.
    - Taller lightning sprites with longer forks.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026. 11. 12
  Graphics:
    - Lightning now curves: each span between corners bows out into an arc. Alternative "smooth"
      and "straight" styles can be picked at the top of control.lua.
    - The number of corners in a bolt now varies each time instead of depending only on distance.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026. 11. 12
  Changes:
    - The mod's own chain lightning is now used with and without Space Age. Space Age still changes
      the recipe, the unlocking technology and which damage research applies.
  Graphics:
    - Lightning now follows a jagged path of random waypoints instead of a straight line. The path
      re-rolls several times a second and follows moving targets.
    - Smaller, tidier lightning sprites built for the shorter path pieces.
    - Spark bursts only at the shooter and the target, not at every corner.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.1
Date: 2026. 11. 12
  Graphics:
    - Lightning arcs are much wider and less orderly: taller sprites, bigger jumps between frames,
      a faint second strand that splits off and rejoins, longer forks and a strobing flicker.
    - Slowed the lightning animation down so it no longer blurs into a solid tube.
    - The ends of the bolt now always touch the shooter and the target.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026. 11. 12
  Graphics:
    - New forking chain lightning sprites when playing without Space Age, replacing the base game's
      electric beam look. The whole bolt jumps around every frame, with spark bursts at the
      shooter and at each target.
